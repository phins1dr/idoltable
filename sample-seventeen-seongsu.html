How to read the labels · 표기 읽는 법
  • verified 근거 확인 — two or more sources agree on the same place and the same detail.
  • single 단일 출처 — one source only. We say so; at most one such stop per route.
  • stale 오래된 근거 — older than two years. Never used in a route.
  • 가 본 곳 one visit confirmed · 자주 간 곳 two or more · 취향으로 묶인 곳 same food type confirmed at three or more places.
  • No “best”, no “top”, no “must-visit”, and no ranking anywhere on the page. The order is a time constraint, not a judgement.

What is placeholder here · what changes with real data
  • Place names, addresses, hours, visit dates, source links and quiz answers are invented for layout review. Nothing on this page is a verified finding.
  • The two badges at the top and every card’s grade are computed from place records in the real build — not typed in by hand.
  • Hours come from the map listing plus the latest official post, and each card prints the date it was checked.
  • Each source becomes its original link plus our captured screen and an archive copy. If the original disappears, the card says so and the capture stays.
  • One single-source stop per route is the cap; a place older than two years leaves the route.
  • Japanese is not translated in this sample. The layout keeps room for the JA line under each English line.

Hours change. Check before you go. Evidence for every place is printed on its card, with the date it was checked.
